I am Devin Whittington

I am a videographer and photographer who wants you to have a good experience and never force you away from anything. Do not take me seriously—but trust me, I take my work seriously. No, I haven't been holding a camera since I was bored as a kid, but I do know how to work professional equipment inside and out. My work isn't about stiff poses or awkward corporate fluff; it's about keeping things relaxed, real, and capturing the moment exactly as it happens. Zero pressure, high impact—you live the experience, and I'll make sure you get comfortable with being on camera.
